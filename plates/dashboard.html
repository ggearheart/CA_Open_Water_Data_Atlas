Federation: one dataset, two catalogs

AB 1755 relies on federation: the same dataset should be findable, and described the same way, on both portals. The portals harvest each other. DWR, CDFW and Ocean Protection Council records flow from CNRA to data.ca.gov, and Water Board records flow the other way through a harvest source CNRA names "AB1755 - CA Water Boards." These charts test how well the copies line up. They ignore the filters above, except Publisher.

Method and caveats

  • Data: CKAN API on both portals (package_search, package_show?include_tracking=true), fetched . Full table: water_datasets.csv. Rebuild with python3 scripts/build_water_dashboard_data.py.
  • "Published water dataset": a record in the portal's Water group. In "Both" mode, a federated pair counts once, and its views are the sum across both copies.
  • Federation match: exact, through harvest metadata (the harvested copy's guid equals the upstream record's id). Unlinked records are also matched on title and publisher.
  • Views: CKAN's built-in tracking counts unique visitors per day on the dataset page. It does not count API calls, direct downloads from external URLs, or ArcGIS service hits, so heavily automated datasets are under-counted. The tracking start date differs by portal and is not published, so compare views within a portal more confidently than across portals.
  • Topic: the first match in an ordered keyword list (see TOPICS in the script). Each dataset gets exactly one topic, and edge cases will be imperfect.
  • Publisher: DWR, State Water Board, CDFW. "Federal" covers federal agency organizations and CNRA's Water Data Partners harvest. "Other" includes the Ocean Protection Council, BCDC, the Delta Stewardship Council and others.
