The catalog grew by federation, once

Almost all of the growth came in 2019, when ocean and federal water records were harvested in to meet the statutory deadlines. Since 2020 the catalog has added about 2,000 records, under 2% a year.

Open questions for the next decade

Is a catalog a platform?

Federation produced findability, not integration. Plan action 3.1.7 called for querying like data through one access point. Nothing does that across agencies yet.

Who funds the plumbing?

The Consortium's 2026 Prop 4 report found that bonds "rarely fund the coordination, governance" behind data. The Act's own fund was never capitalized.

Program systems vs. the platform

The decade's largest water-data investment, water-rights modernization (UPWARD/CalWATRS, a reported $60M over two budgets), came through the Water Board's own program, not AB 1755. Is AB 1755 now the publishing layer for program systems rather than a system of its own?

A plan update

The plan was to be refreshed with each Water Plan. SB 72 launched Water Plan 2028 in February 2026, which is a natural vehicle.

Amending the Act

AB 2728 (2026) was introduced to add hatchery data and a 2027 deadline. It was chaptered on Sept 30, 2026 under a groundwater-fees title, so the AB 1755 amendments appear to have been dropped. Unconfirmed.

Standards

Ten protocol topics listed in 2018 are still "to be developed," including interoperability, QC, federation and security. The Internet of Water's ingestion frameworks offer a template.
