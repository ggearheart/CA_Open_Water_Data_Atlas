An atlas in seven plates

California Open Water Data Atlas

In 2016 California passed the Open and Transparent Water Data Act (AB 1755), promising one integrated, open platform for the state's water and ecological data. This atlas maps the decade since: what the law required, what the 2018 Strategic Plan set out to do, and where things stand today.
